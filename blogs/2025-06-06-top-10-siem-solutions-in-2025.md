The SIEM landscape is evolving fast. With threats growing in volume and complexity, security teams, especially those with small SecOps teams, are under pressure to detect and respond faster with fewer resources. Legacy SIEMs fall short: they’re expensive, noisy, and require significant overhead to manage.
