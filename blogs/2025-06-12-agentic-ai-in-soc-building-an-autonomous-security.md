Under constant pressure to do more with less, security operations teams are turning to AI as the long-promised solution. But here’s the catch: most AI offerings today don’t solve the problem - they just repackage it. Despite all the hype around “AI-SOC analysts," many tools stop at co-pilot functionality: summarizing alerts, translating queries, or responding to prompts.
